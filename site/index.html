Motivation & Methodology

Why Grammar Induction Over Black-Box Models?

Traditional hand-crafted parsers require expert compiler engineering, while bag-of-words / neural classifiers suffer high false acceptance and cannot construct parse trees or localize syntax errors.

⚠️ Why Bag-of-Words & Classifiers Fall Short

Classifiers evaluate n-gram token co-occurrences without structural grammar awareness. Malformed queries like SELECT * FROM marks WHERE marks_obtained > = 60; fool statistical classifiers into predicting 84% valid likelihood because all words are familiar.

83.99% False Acceptance Rate

✓ Why Automatic Grammar Induction Succeeds

AutoGrammar mines syntactic skeletons from valid training queries, abstracts identifiers/literals, and generates verifiable Context-Free EBNF productions executed by deterministic Earley parsers.

Examiner FAQ

Limitations & Viva Q&A Accordion

Honest, defensible answers to the 12 most critical technical questions expected during project review.

Known Project Limitations:
  • Single Train/Test Split: Results are reported on a single 70/15/15 seed-42 partition rather than 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Synthetic Data: Ground truth queries are template-generated rather than collected from live student logs.
  • Grammar Expressiveness: Supports core SELECT/JOIN/DML subsets; full ANSI SQL-99 (CTEs, window functions) is intentionally excluded.
